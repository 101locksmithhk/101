Service Promise

1 No Success, No Service Fee

If our locksmith arrives but cannot open the specified lock, we will not charge the unlocking service fee, travel fee or call-out fee. This gives you clear protection before work begins.

2 Clear Quotes, No Random Add-ons

Upfront quote: We quote clearly by phone or WhatsApp and do not inflate the price afterwards.
No hidden charges: Unless the actual situation is materially different from the description, we will not add extra fees without reason.

3 Skilled Unlocking, Damage-Free First

Damage-free principle: We prioritise skilled unlocking and aim to avoid damage to cylinders, doors and frames.
Before destructive work: If damage cannot be avoided, the locksmith will explain it again before proceeding.

4 Fast Arrival, 24-Hour Support

Response time: We aim to arrive within 30 minutes in most Hong Kong districts after accepting the job.
365-day support: Emergency service is available during public holidays and late-night hours.

5 Professional Ethics and Privacy

Identity checks: We verify that the customer has the right to enter before unlocking.
Professional integrity: We do not retain key data and keep lock security details confidential.

6 After-Service Warranty

Replacement warranty: Door locks include 14 days of support. Digital locks carry brand-dependent original warranty, usually up to 2 years. We remain available after installation.
