Common situations and precautions for magnetic locks

  • Automatically locks when closing the door: The magnetic lock relies on magnetic adsorption and automatically pops up when the door is closed. If the key is left in the room, it will be easily locked.
  • Put the key in the toilet: This situation is very common. It is recommended to develop a habit of carrying the key with you or placing it in a fixed location.
  • It is not easy to forcefully open the door: The magnetic lock has a precise structure. Forcibly prying the door may damage the lock body and the door frame, increasing maintenance costs.
  • Professional skills in unlocking: 101 locksmith can use professional tools and techniques to open the door lock without damaging the door lock. The door lock can be used normally after unlocking.
