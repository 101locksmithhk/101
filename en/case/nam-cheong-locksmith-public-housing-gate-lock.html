Common situations and precautions for gate locks in public housing estates

  • Automatically locks when closing the door: Most metal gate locks in public housing are designed with automatic locks. The lock tongue will automatically pop out when the door is closed. If the key is left in the house, it will be locked.
  • Forgot to bring the key: This situation is very common. It is recommended to develop a habit of checking whether the key is with you before going out, or to leave a backup key with a reliable relative or friend.
  • It is not easy to forcefully open the door: The gate lock structure of public housing is relatively standard. Forcibly prying the door may damage the lock cylinder and gate frame, increasing maintenance costs and procedures.
  • Professional skills in unlocking and retaining the lock cylinder: 101 locksmiths can use professional tools to open the lock without damaging the lock cylinder. After the lock is opened, the lock cylinder can be used normally and does not need to be replaced.

Professional advice: If you accidentally leave the key in the house and the metal gate is locked, Qianqi should not pry the door or open it violently.
