Wan Chai Wooden Door Emergency Unlocking Case｜Diamond Wooden Door Three Unlocking Technology

The wooden door of the guest's residence uses Diamond wooden door three-level unlocking. Due to the urgent need to enter the house, he contacted 101 Locksmith to arrange emergency unlocking. After the customer provides the door lock photo and location via WhatsApp, 101 Lock will first make a preliminary quotation based on the type and condition of the lock, and then arrange for a master to come to the door to pick the lock using technical unlocking methods.

The reason why the customer asked for unlocking

The guest needs to open the wooden door of the residence due to emergency, and the door lock is Diamond brand. Since the on-site need to enter the house as soon as possible, the guest does not want to pry the door or force it by himself to avoid damaging the door, damaging the lock cylinder or making the door lock more complicated, so contact 101 to arrange for a locksmith to arrive on site.

Main concerns of customers

Guests are most worried about the wooden door being damaged, the door lock having to be replaced immediately, or the original key being no longer usable after unlocking. The master first checks the Diamond wooden door's three-opening lock structure and lock cylinder status. After confirming that the technical unlocking technology is suitable, the master will pick the lock and retain the original door lock.

101 Locksmith and maintenance suggestions

When emergency unlocking a wooden door, it is recommended to first send photos of the door lock, the location of the door and the on-site situation, so that the master can judge the technical unlocking method. The door lock can operate normally after completing the test and does not need to be replaced immediately. If the key jam, idling or slow rebound of the lock tongue occurs in the future, it should be checked as soon as possible.
