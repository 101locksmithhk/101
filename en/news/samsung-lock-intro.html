Samsung digital lock cannot open the door?

Follow the steps to save yourself immediately | 9V battery emergency power supply | Professional maintenance support

1. Samsung digital locks are extremely popular in Hong Kong, but suddenly they cannot be opened?

Samsung Digital Door Lock has become a mainstream choice for new buildings and renovations in Hong Kong with its stylish design and convenience. However, digital locks always rely on electricity and electronic components, and it often happens that the door cannot be opened suddenly - most of them are not broken, but because the battery is dead, the lock is locked, or the setting is wrong. As long as you master the correct steps, many problems can be solved instantly without having to hire a locksmith to help.

Common reasons:
1. The battery is exhausted or the voltage is insufficient (about 70% of cases)
2. The fingerprint sensor is dirty or the finger is too wet/too dry
3. Lock the machine
4. The lock tongue is mechanically stuck (deformation of the door frame or weather effects)

2. Samsung digital lock cannot open the door - step-by-step self-rescue guide

Keep calm and follow the following steps to check. If you have a chance, you can open the door yourself without waiting for the master.

  • Get the spare mechanical key —Most Samsung digital locks have a hidden mechanical keyhole, usually at the bottom of the lock body or under the sliding cover. Opening the door directly with the backup key is the safest method. It is recommended to keep the backup key in the house or office of a reliable relative or friend.
  • Use 9V battery/urine bag for emergency power supply — Go to a convenience store to buy a 9V alkaline battery/rental urine bag. If the emergency power supply is provided by a 9V battery, find two metal contact points, and press the positive and negative electrodes of the battery against the two contacts for 5-10 seconds; if the emergency power supply is provided by a urine bag, insert the fork after checking the potential. Immediately after hearing the sound of music, use the registered password, fingerprint and card to unlock. Replace all 8 new alkaline batteries immediately after entering the house.
    • Important reminder: If the key can be twisted but cannot open the door, or the key is stuck after being twisted, the internal parts of the lock body may be faulty. Do not twist forcibly, otherwise the lock body may be damaged. Please contact 101 Locksmith immediately.

4. Precautions for Samsung digital locks

The battery must be alkaline
Recommendation: Only use high-quality alkaline batteries (Energizer, Duracell). It is strictly forbidden to mix old and new batteries or different brands, otherwise it will easily leak and corrode the circuit board, and even cause the lock to be scrapped.
Keep the sensor clean
Use a soft dry cloth to wipe the fingerprint reader and PIN pad regularly. Do not use alcohol or detergent to avoid damaging the coating.
You must bring a backup mechanical key
It is recommended that you always keep the key with you. If you really don’t want to carry a key, you can consider placing the backup key in a reliable relative or friend’s house or car. When there is a problem with the digital lock, you can rely on the emergency key to enter the house, avoiding wasting money on hiring a locksmith.
Do not disassemble the lock body parts by yourself
Once the lock body parts are damaged, it is difficult to restore them. In addition, there are maintenance stickers to protect the screws when removing the lock body. Once the stickers are damaged, maintenance will be lost.
