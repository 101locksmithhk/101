American lock cylinder decryption: screw cylinder vs pin cylinder

Fixation methods, maintenance suggestions, DIY replacement guide

1. The mainstream status of American locks in Hong Kong

Hong Kong residences and even village houses are often seenAmerican Mortise Lockappears. As the core part, the lock cylinder is mainly divided into two camps:「Screw bladder」 Same as 「Pin insert」. Although the appearance of the two is similar, the fixing methods are completely different. Many owners/tenants buy the wrong lock cylinder and end up with the new lock cylinder being wasted. Some even fail to replace it and the lock body is scrapped. This article will provide an in-depth analysis of the differences between the two types of lock cylinders to help you make smart repairs.

2. Screw bladder vs. pin bladder: The structural differences are clear at a glance

Schematic diagram of screw bladderActual product picture: Fixing screw holes visible on the side
Screw bladder

Fixed method: There is a recess on the side of the lock cylinder, which can be directly tightened and fixed by a transverse screw on the lock body. Just loosen the screws when replacing.

Advantages: High popularity, easy to get at hardware stores; replacement cost is low.

Disadvantages: If the screws become loose over time, the lock cylinder may shake. Replacement requires skills.

Pin diagramActual product picture: pin fixing structure
Pin tube

Fixed method: The lock cylinder is fixed with a latch and is not exposed. You only need to pull it out when replacing it.

Advantages: Sturdy and durable, easy to replace, and no risk of screw loosening.

Disadvantages: The popularity is low and difficult to buy/distribute in general hardware stores; the replacement cost is high.

Summary of core differences: The screw bladder is fixed by screws; the pin bladder is locked by pins. The two are by no means universal, so be sure to confirm the type of lock cylinder you own before purchasing.

Regardless of whether the screws or pins are used, different problems will occur. The following is a summary of common faults based on Hong Kong locksmith repair experience:

4. DIY lock cylinder replacement must-read: Screw cylinder vs pin cylinder precautions

Key points for screw bladder replacement
1. First loosen the fixing screw on the side of the door lock body (on the horizontal side of the lock cylinder)
2. Twist out the old lock cylinder and measure the length of the lock cylinder (common sizes are inch 2/inch 3/inch 5/inch 2 inch 2)
3. Purchase screws of the same size
4. The screws must be tightened after installation, but avoid excessive force to slide the threads.
Pin cartridge replacement warning
1. There are no exposed screws in the pin bladder, and the fixed pin is hidden inside the lock body.
2. Remove the old lock cylinder and measure the length of the lock cylinder
3. Purchase screw bladders of the same size. Hardware stores in the market retail pin bladders with fewer models, so you may need to purchase them from a lock shop or professional locksmith.
4. After installation, the pin must be inserted tightly, but avoid inserting it in the wrong position.
101 Lock Picking Tips: Not sure if you have screws or pins? You can look into the lock body from the side of the door: if you see a screw, it is a screw bladder, and if you see a pin, it is a pin bladder. If you have any doubts, you can send it via WhatsApp so we can identify it for free.

7. Physical comparison of lock body: pin cylinder lock body vs screw cylinder lock body

The lock body structure is the factor that determines the side lock cylinder. The two structures are completely different. The pictures below show the actual differences between the two lock bodies. You can avoid buying the wrong parts if you understand the characteristics first.

Special lock body for pin lock cylinderFeatures: No screw holes, the lock cylinder is inserted and fixed with a pin
Pin lock body
  • There are no screws to fix the lock cylinder on the lock body.
  • The latch is clearly visible
  • Commonly found in some housing estates original locks
Special lock body for screw bladderFeatures: Visible screw holes, use screws to tighten the lock cylinder
Screw lock body
  • There are screw fixing holes on the side or directly below the lock cylinder.
  • Only a screwdriver is needed for replacement, DIY is easy
  • Common configurations of mainstream door lock brands in Hong Kong (such as YALE, MUL-T-LOCK, Bonco)
How to distinguish quickly? Looking at the side of the door lock body:If there are screws, it is a screw bladder system; if there are only pins and no screws, it is a pin bladder system.。
