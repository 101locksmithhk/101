Three unlocks and three-stage locks

Structure, usage, modification, installation dimensions | Hong Kong old public housing/stainless steel door only

Origin of the name Three-segment structure

Three unlocksis named after its internal structure is divided intoThree paragraphs: One-stage inclined stroke + two-stage square bolt. Three stages operate independently, providing double security protection of "automatic locking + manual strengthening".

Common installation locations

Hong Kong old-style public housing wooden doors, stainless steel doors, metal gates, gates, etc. Because of its ruggedness and reliability, it is still widely used today.

Slant + both sides of the latch Automatically lock

The inclined tongue is responsible for closing the door and automatically locking it; the two sections of the square latch can be extended with a key or indoor twister to provide additional anti-theft strength. The three sections are independent and do not interfere with each other.

Installation dimensions

Lock center: 60mm (distance from the center of the lock cylinder to the edge of the lock body)
Lock hole: 32mm (door side opening diameter)
Applicable to most standard old door reserved holes.

Outdoor usage (outdoor operation)

Lock the door when going out
  • 1. Close the door → Slope automatically locks (first paragraph)
  • 2. For more security, use the key to turn the key clockwise.Twice → Extend two sections of square bolt (second and third sections)
    • ※ Only the first section of the square latch will be extended by twisting it once, and it will be completely locked by twisting it twice.
Open the door when entering the house
  • Method 1 (fully unlocked)：Key screwTwice → Retract the two square bolts one after another; then tightenThe third time → Retract the slope and open the door.
  • Method 2 (quickly open the door): Tighten directly → Only retract the diagonal lever (assuming the square bolt is not extended), and then the door can be pushed.
  • The actual situation depends on whether the square latch is locked. If it only relies on the automatic lock with a tilt, the door can be opened by twisting it.

Indoor usage (indoor operation)

Stay / Ordinary lock door
  • Close the door → Slope automatically locks (same as outdoor)
  • For extra safety, place indoorwrist handsScrewTwice → Extend the two sections of the square latch and lock it completely.
  • ※ Twisting the hand can only control the square latch, while the tilting lever remains in the automatic state.
Leave indoors
  • First place the indoorTwist twice → Retract the two-stage square latch
  • againHold hands → Retract the slope and open the door at the same time
  • The whole process is fast and smooth, and no keys are required.

Comparison of indoor twisting hands

  • Stay/Normal Lock: close the door to lock the latch automatically; turn the thumbturnTwice → the deadbolt extends fully for greater security.
  • Leave the room: Twist your handsTwiceto retract the deadbolt → pull the handle once to retract the latch → open the door.
  • Emergency: if only the latch is locked, pull the handle directly to open the door; the thumbturn is not required.
Safety reminder: If the metal gate uses a standard three-way lock (indoor twist handle), a thief may use an iron wire to hook the twist handle from the gate slit to open the lock. It is recommended that the metal gate be replaced with a "modified indoor lock cylinder" three-opening lock, which must be opened with a key first to eliminate the risk of unlocking the hanger.

Installation dimensions and precautions

Standard size:

  • Backset:60mm
  • Lock hole diameter:32mm
  • Applicable door thickness: 35mm – 50mm (common wooden doors/stainless steel doors)

Replacement tips:

  • Before replacing the three-opening lock, please confirm whether the distance between the locks is 60mm, otherwise you need to re-drill the hole.
  • If the original lock system is the old-style "gourd gallbladder", most of the three-opening locks are compatible.
  • It is recommended to hire a professional locksmith to install it, ensuring that the angled and square bolts are aligned with the door frame.
FAQAnswer
Will the three-way lock automatically lock when closing the door?Yes, the tilt tongue has an automatic pop-up function and locks when the door is closed. Square latches require manual locking with a key or indoor twister.
Why do you sometimes open the door just by turning the key outside?Because you only relied on the oblique automatic lock before, and did not lock the upper latch. Just twist and retract the slope to open the door.
How does Tie Run use three locks to prevent "clothes hanger unlocking"?Choose the "modified indoor lock cylinder" version. You need to use the key to open the indoor lock first, and it cannot be opened with a wire from the outside.
What is the difference between a three-way lock and an ordinary door lock?General door locks usually only have an oblique angle + one section of square bolt; a three-opening lock has two sections of square bolt, which has stronger anti-theft power and clear structure.
