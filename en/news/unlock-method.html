Comprehensive review of unlocking methods

Technology, trickery, and destruction—the three unique skills of a locksmith’s toolbox (detailed deconstruction)

Technical unlocking Lock Pick

Principle: Use lily keys, crochet hooks and other tools to move the lower beads in the lock cylinder one by one to simulate the state when the original lock key is inserted and make the lock cylinder rotate. It does not damage the door lock structure at all.

Common tools
  • Hook Pick
  • Tension Wrench
  • Bump Key
  • Electric lock picking gun
  • Decoding tool
Applicability

Traditional mechanical lock; the customer wants to keep the original door lock; the locksmith has a skilled touch.

Open the lock without damage and keep the original key requires extremely high skills, and complex lock cylinders require a long time

Ghostly unlock Bypass

Principle: Bypass the lock cylinder and use loopholes in the door lock design or mechanical weaknesses to open the door, such as credit card prying tongues or cat-eye tools to pull the internal handle.

Common methods
  • Credit card/film unlocking
  • Inflatable air bag + long pole
  • Cat’s Eye Tool
  • Magnetic interference with old electric locks
Applicability

The door lock installation has loopholes, a latch bolt, and a large door gap; in an emergency, the door must be opened as quickly as possible.

Very fast, a few seconds to a minute Only suitable for locks with design flaws, not valid for modern high security locks

Destroy unlocking Destroy

Principle: Use violence to directly damage the lock body or door structure, such as drilling through the lock cylinder and cutting off the lock tongue. Last resort.

Common methods
  • The electric drill destroys the lock cylinder
  • Mill cutting lock tongue
  • Pry open violently
  • Hydraulic shears
Applicability

The lock cylinder is severely corroded, the digital lock is shut down, there is a fire, or someone is trapped and the door is broken open in an emergency.

100% direct, regardless of lock type Destroy the door lock and need to replace it with a new lock afterwards, which may damage the door/door frame
