Let’s talk about the conclusion first: three-opening locks may not necessarily be directly replaced by tiger locks

"Direct replacement" refers to installing a new lock body under the door without significantly changing the door body; whether it is feasible depends on the size of the actual door and lock, rather than relying solely on the name "three-opening lock".

It is suitable to consider the situation of tiger lock first:The existing door has a three-stage deadbolt structure. There is enough space for the lock body position and door thickness, and the deadbolt can be aligned with the door frame lock. Even if the appearance is similar, the openings, left and right hands, and lock distances of different brands or old doors may be different. It is recommended to confirm with a locksmith first.

Why is it called tiger lock?

"Tiger lock" is a commonly used name for this type of three-stage deadbolt lock body in the market, because its tongue bite structure gives people the impression of being as powerful as a tiger. Some models can be equipped with hardened steel bars or anti-saw bolts to improve their resistance to vandalism. The actual security effect still depends on the lock body, lock cylinder, door frame and installation quality. You cannot just look at the lock name or promotional words.

Check these 3 things before changing your locks

These three points are the main variables that affect the difficulty of installing the tiger lock, whether the door can be modified less, and the final quotation.

1. Door type and door opening direction

The structures of wooden doors, stainless steel doors and fire doors are different; left-hand, right-hand and inside-outside opening will also affect the optional lock body and installation direction. The structure of sliding doors is generally not suitable for modifying tiger locks and must be confirmed individually.

2. Lock distance, opening and door thickness

60mm locking distance and 32mm opening are common specifications for tiger locks, but they are not universal specifications for all tiger locks. The lock cylinder hole, hand pull position and door thickness should be checked together.

3. Align the door frame and lock

The most important thing after changing the lock is that the lock tongue is properly engaged. If the door frame is deformed or there is a problem with the door hinge falling, it should be adjusted first; you cannot just rely on changing the lock to solve the alignment problem.

The benefits of tiger locks should be understood according to the model and installation conditions

High security functions are not available in every tiger lock; when purchasing, please refer to the actual lock cylinder, lock body and construction method.

Possible upgrades

  • The three-stage operation logic of oblique tongue and square tongue is retained, and the habit is easier to continue.
  • You can choose a stronger lock body, lock cylinder or indoor key operation configuration according to the model.
  • If the original hole position is compatible with the lock distance, there is an opportunity to reduce the door modification project.

Things that should not be guaranteed directly

  • It cannot be assumed that all three unlocks do not require door modification.
  • It cannot be assumed that all doors can be modified with tiger locks; especially for sliding doors, the structure and feasibility need to be confirmed first.
  • The "anti-drilling/anti-pry" statement on the lock brand should not be taken as a guarantee that all models are equipped with it.
  • The price will be confirmed based on the photo quotation received from the customer; the working hours and final results must also be confirmed based on the door condition and model.

The sizes commonly found online are only for preliminary reference. Since the specifications of the tiger lock will vary depending on the version and model, the actual quantity of the existing door lock and the description of the new lock should prevail.

Tiger lock FAQ

If the door is in special condition or is a fire door, please refer to the relevant management requirements.

Is it possible to replace the tiger lock with three unlocks?

Not necessarily. Although some tiger locks can be equipped with a three-stage deadbolt structure, the door type, door opening direction, lock distance, old hole position, door thickness and door frame lock will all affect it. Check the appearance or measurements first to confirm whether it is a direct replacement.

What photos need to be uploaded before quoting?

It is recommended to send: ① Photos of the outdoor door lock after the door is opened, ② Photos of the door lock body after the door is opened, ③ Photos of the indoor door lock after the door is opened. Photos can speed up the initial evaluation and quote.

Is a tiger lock necessarily better than an digital lock?

No. Tiger locks and digital locks solve different needs: the former is more suitable for people who want to retain the operating habits of mechanical locks and three-opening locks; digital locks focus on keyless, password or access management. Before choosing, you should consider the door condition, user needs and budget.
