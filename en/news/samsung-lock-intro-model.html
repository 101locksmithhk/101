1. Samsung digital lock｜No.1 smart lock market share in Hong Kong

Samsung Digital Door Lock has become the first choice for new properties and home decoration in Hong Kong due to its stylish design, multiple unlocking methods and stability and durability. Whether it is a private housing estate, a village house or a commercial building, Samsung digital locks are the most common smart lock brand. In order to facilitate users' purchase, Samsung divides its products into three series:Push-Pull、Lever Handle and Additional lock (Bolt-Lock), the following details are introduced one by one.

Must read before purchasing:
Confirm the thickness of the door (usually 45-60mm) and the size of the lock (70mm)
Understand the door opening method you need (fingerprint/password/sensor card/Bluetooth/WiFi)

4. 3 Tips for Choosing Samsung digital locks

1. Confirm door specifications
• Door thickness: Most require 45-60mm
• Lock size: mainstream 70mm
• Door opening direction
2. Select functions according to living habits
• Often forget to bring the key → Fingerprint model
• Remote door opening is required → WiFi model
• With children/pets → Safety handles
