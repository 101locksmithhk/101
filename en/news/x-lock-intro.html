Can't the cross lock key be inserted?

Detailed reason disassembly, daily maintenance, marking alignment teaching

1. How common are cross locks in Hong Kong?

Cross locks (also known as cross locks) are door locks commonly used in old-style tenement buildings, private building gates, metal gates and many offices in Hong Kong. The cross section of the lock cylinder is cross-shaped, with four rows of beads corresponding to the grooves on the four sides of the key. Although digital locks have become popular in recent years, cross locks are still widely used because of their simplicity and simplicity of installation. The most common question is"The key cannot be inserted" or "It is stuck halfway", many neighbors will be trapped outside because of this.

Features: The cross lock key is symmetrical on all four sides, but in fact some cross locks have direction restrictions, and there will be "marks" on the key to indicate the correct insertion direction. Many people ignore the markings and insert them randomly, causing the marbles to become misaligned. Over time, the key cannot be inserted and even the lock cylinder is damaged.

2. Cross key mark teaching: insertion/extraction must be aligned with the normal position

Many cross keys have a tinymark (dimple, arrow, color dot or engraved line), this mark means "up" or "toward the specified direction". Before inserting, the mark must be aligned with the reference point on the lock cylinder housing (usually the scale or dot on the lock cylinder), so that it can be inserted and pulled out smoothly. If the mark is misaligned, forced insertion will accelerate wear and tear, or even cause instant jamming.

Figure 1: Key mark location
The common bumps/pits (inside the red circle) on cross keys are the marks of the "normal insertion direction".
Figure 2: Lock cylinder reference point
The lock cylinder usually has a small dot or engraved line. When inserting the key, align the mark on the key with this reference point.
Figure 3: Correct insertion method Align the
mark upwards with the reference point of the lock cylinder, push it gently to the bottom, and then twist the key first. Always keep the mark in the same direction when pulling it out.
Important reminder: Regardless of whether the key is inserted or pulled out, the mark should be kept aligned with the reference position of the lock cylinder. If the lock is forcibly inserted or pulled out when the mark is misaligned, the marble inside the lock cylinder will be compressed and deformed, and a "key cannot be inserted" or "key cannot be pulled out" failure will occur soon.

4. How to prevent the cross key from not being inserted? 6 tips for simple maintenance

Get used to inserting/unplugging tags
Before each use, confirm that the key mark is aligned with the reference point of the lock cylinder. Developing the correct orientation habit can extend the life of the lock cylinder.
Avoid using the key to push/throw open the door
Make good use of door handles and avoid using keys to open doors.
Avoid violent insertion of keys
There is obstruction when inserting the key, so do not force it. If resistance is found, pull it out immediately and check.
Avoid matching keys
The cross key has four sides, which naturally increases the difficulty of key matching. Avoid mismatching. Use original keys instead to reduce errors.
Correctly install the dust cover
A dust-proof cover can be added to the cross lock outside the door to prevent rain and dust from directly entering the lock hole and causing rust or scale accumulation.
Pay attention to key deformation/blurred markings
If the key is bent or the mark is so worn that it cannot be seen clearly, a new key must be obtained immediately. Do not reuse it, otherwise the cross lock may be damaged.
