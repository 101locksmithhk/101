What does TSA key leakage mean, and why not try online methods?

Public reports in 2015 discussed leaked TSA master-key images, followed by online copies and product discussions. This shows TSA locks have limits, but it does not mean travellers should try to open locks themselves.

Do not copy online opening methods

Online content about TSA keys, TSA lock bypassing or combination lock opening can lead people to pry, cut or pull too hard. That may deform the case and damage items inside. If you want to preserve the suitcase, ask the brand after-sales team or a locksmith for assessment first.

Luggage lock and TSA lock FAQ

Can 101 Locksmith help if my suitcase key is lost?

You can first send WhatsApp photos of the suitcase, lock area, Hong Kong location and problem description. We will ask for reasonable ownership proof before assessing whether we can help.

What should I do if my TSA lock will not open?

Do not force the lock, cut the zipper or pull the latch hard. Check brand support first; if you need access urgently in Hong Kong, contact 101 Locksmith for an initial assessment.

Do I always need to cut the lock if I forgot the luggage code?

No. It depends on the suitcase brand, lock type, latch condition and existing damage. Send photos first and do not break the lock yourself.

Which Hong Kong areas can ask about luggage lock opening?

Hong Kong Island, Kowloon, New Territories, hotels, residences, offices, malls and airport-area enquiries can all be checked first.

Where is the TSA key for a new suitcase?

Many three-digit TSA locks do not include a traveller key. The keyway is for authorised screening personnel. Travellers normally use the combination code.

What is a TSA lock?

A TSA lock is a luggage lock with Travel Sentry or related recognition. It is mainly designed so authorised screening personnel can open and relock checked luggage when required.
