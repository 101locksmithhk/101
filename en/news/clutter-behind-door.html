Let’s talk about the conclusion first

If the door cannot be opened, it does not necessarily mean that the door lock is broken.

Living space in Hong Kong is limited, and many families place wooden ladders, vacuum cleaners, shoe boxes, tool boxes or furniture behind the door. If objects are displaced due to collision, unstable placement or vibration, they may push against the door, causing the door to only open slightly or even not open at all.

Why do wooden ladders, vacuum cleaners, washing machines and cabinets get stuck?

Wooden ladder and long tool

Dangerous causes:When a folding ladder, ironing board, floor mop or broom is upright against the wall, it may fall into the door's range of motion due to collision or unstable placement.

Prevention:Put it flat in a suitable storage space, or secure it with a stable hanger and safety buckle.

Vacuum cleaner

Dangerous causes:If the upright vacuum cleaner or the body with pulleys is close to the door, it may tip or slide against the bottom of the door or the door leaf.

Prevention:Store it in the designated location, ensuring that the body and pipe are not in the opening path of the door.

Washing machines and large electrical appliances

Dangerous causes:If the washing machine is not leveled, the ground is unstable, or it is close to the door, continuous vibration during operation may cause the position to change.

Prevention:Level the machine feet according to the product instructions; if it continues to shift, please ask a suitable person to check the installation and ground conditions.

Cabinet buckets, shoe racks and cartons

Dangerous causes:The cabinet bucket is not completely closed, or the cartons, shoe racks, toys and pet supplies fall out, which may block the door or door.

Prevention:Avoid stacking objects behind the door, and make sure the cabinet bucket near the door can remain closed.

Home anti-card guide

Keep the opening range of exits, passages and doors clear

Storage principles

  • Wooden ladders, ironing boards and long-handled items should be placed flat or firmly fixed.
  • Vacuum cleaners, floor mops and brooms should be kept away from the door's swing range.
  • Avoid stacking objects that are easy to fall behind the door, especially do not stack high cardboard boxes.

Electrical appliances and furniture

  • Make sure the electrical appliances near the door are placed stably and will not block the exit.
  • If you find that the washing machine is shifting repeatedly, you should first check the leveling and installation.
  • Cabinet buckets, movable shelves or pet fences near the door must be kept fixed.

Two minutes per month

Behind Door Safety Checklist

  • Completely open and close the main door and common room doors once, and confirm that there are no debris in the opening range.
  • Check whether the cabinet barrel is completely closed and whether there are any drawers that can slide out easily.
  • Make sure there is a safe distance between the washer, dryer or refrigerator and the door.
  • Whether stepladders, long tools, shoe boxes and cartons are placed securely.
  • Whether pet supplies, toys or movable playpens may enter the door's opening and closing path.

Four common situations where debris in the house gets stuck

These scenarios are used to assist judgment and prevention. They do not represent verified cases in designated housing estates, nor can they guarantee the processing time or method in advance.

Scenario 1

The stepladder collapsed and the door could only be opened a small amount

The folding ladder is placed upright behind the door. If it is bumped or tilted sideways due to unstable placement, it may hit the inward opening door. The focus of prevention is to lay the ladder flat or secure it firmly.

Scenario 2

The washing machine shifts, blocking the opening path of the door

The washing machine is close to the door and vibrates repeatedly. It may gradually enter the door leaf range. The leveling should be checked according to the product instructions first; if it continues to shift, arrange for inspection by a suitable person.

Scenario 3

The cabinet bucket pops out and the door cannot be pushed open from the outside

If the cabinet bucket near the door is not closed properly or slides out on its own, the protruding part may hit the door. Do not push the door harder when you don't know what's behind it, to avoid damaging the furniture or causing it to get stuck more tightly.

Scenario 4

Vacuum cleaner or cleaning tool gets stuck

Upright vacuum cleaners, floor mops or brooms are leaning against the door. Once they fall down, the body, pulleys or long handles may block the door leaf.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about door being blocked by debris

The door cannot be opened. Is it possible that the door lock is broken?

Not necessarily. Deformation of door locks, door frames, hinges or door body may cause problems; wooden ladders, vacuum cleaners, cartons, cabinets or moving appliances may also block door leaves. Actual clues should be recorded first, rather than making your own conclusions.

The door is blocked by debris and cannot be opened. Can I break the door by myself?

Not recommended. Forcing the door against the door may cause the obstruction to become tighter and damage the door frame.

The cabinet bucket behind the door is stuck. Do I have to break the door lock?

Not necessarily. Whether it can be handled non-destructively depends on the direction of the door, the location of obstacles, the door gap, the condition of the lock and door frame, and whether there has been any forced operation before. Plans should be evaluated first and then confirmed.
