Which type of key breaking situation do you belong to?

A

The door can still be opened, and a small amount of the broken key is exposed

Do not turn the lock cylinder yet. If the broken key is obviously exposed, you can only gently pinch it out in the direction of the key's entry and exit. If it gets stuck when you pull it, stop immediately to avoid metal deformation.

B

The door is locked and the broken key is completely submerged

This is a high-risk situation. Do not insert a spare key, use a swastika clip, or add glue to the keyhole. It is recommended to check emergency unlocking and broken key removal after taking pictures.

C

The broken key has been taken out, but the lock cylinder is not smooth

The broken original key cannot be used anymore. You should check whether the lock cylinder is worn and whether the marble is stuck, and then decide to use a spare key, rekey or replace the lock cylinder.

Avoid secondary damage

4 things you must not do if the key breaks inside the lock cylinder.

The following actions may not cause the lock cylinder to be scrapped every time, but it will indeed greatly increase the difficulty of removal and maintenance costs. Especially when you can no longer get into the house, the more anxious you are, the more urgent it is to stop.

1

Do not use AA glue, hot melt glue or tape to cut it out

Glue may flow into the inside of the lock cylinder, causing the marble and spring to get stuck. Even if the broken key is finally removed, the lock cylinder may still become jammed and require disassembly or replacement.

2

Do not poke with sharp objects, swastika clips or hard needles

Without angles and fulcrums, most sharp objects will only push the broken key deeper. After the broken key is completely flat or submerged, continuing to try will usually only make it more difficult to handle with professional tools.

3

Do not insert the spare key to push or try to open the door again

Another key may tilt the broken key, clamp it, or scratch the lock cylinder. While the door is still locked, this type of operation may also cause the lock cylinder that could have been retained to need to be replaced.

4

Do not add excess lubricant

A small amount of proper lubrication sometimes helps, but spraying oil randomly in an emergency may mix dust and make the lock cylinder stickier. Taking photos and checking first is safer than adding ingredients blindly.

When should you immediately stop trying to open the lock yourself?

The door is locked, the broken key cannot be seen at all, the key has been stuck before and cannot be turned, glue or hard objects have been tried, or the gate/company door lock is involved. None of these situations are suitable for continuing to handle on your own.
