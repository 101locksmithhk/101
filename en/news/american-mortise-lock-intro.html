Comprehensive deconstruction of American locks

Large draw lock・Parts・Trouble・DIY precautions

1. How common are American-style locks in Hong Kong?

In recent years, American-style set locks have become very popular in Hong Kong, and many new buildings have chosen them as the handover standard. This type of lock is often called「Big Lock」, because it has a larger hand pump on the outer panel, it feels more stable and high-end.

Main features: Automatic locking
After closing the door, the lock tongue will automatically pop up and lock. To enter again, you must use the key. This design is very convenient, but it also means that you must carry the key with you, otherwise you will easily get locked out.

4. What should you pay attention to when DIY repair or replacement?

American-style locks have many accessories to choose from, which makes them easier to replace, but it also makes it easier to get caught. If you want to replace the lock cylinder or lock body yourself in Hong Kong, there are a few points you should pay special attention to:

Distinguish between "screw bladders" and "pin bladders"
The most popular lock cylinder is the "screw cylinder" (fixed with screws), but some housing estates use "pin cylinders" (fixed with pins), and the two are not universal at all. Before buying a lock cylinder, it is best to remove the old lock cylinder to confirm how it is fixed.
Confirm the "locked" size
The lock body has an important dimension called "lock center", which refers to the distance from the edge of the door to the center of the door lock panel. The mainstream in Hong Kong is 70mm, but some housing estates will choose 90mm or even 100mm is neither a standard size. Be sure to measure the size before replacement, or take photos and consult a locksmith.
Confirm the left and right directions of the lock body
The lock bodies of some brands are available for left and right hands (that is, diagonal and sharp directions). When purchasing, be sure to note that buying in the wrong direction cannot be solved by reinstalling the lock body, which will directly affect the user experience and appearance.
Don't misuse "lock body" to fix "lock cylinder" problems/"lock cylinder" to fix "lock body" problems
This is the most common misunderstanding among customers. Determining the value of a locksmith is the first step in solving the problem.
