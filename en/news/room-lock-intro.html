Channel lock Passage / Closet

Inside - Bobo Outside - Bobo
The latch tongue can be opened by both internal and external waves, and there is no locking function at all. Suitable for stairs, kitchens, fire escapes or corridors where privacy is not required.

Automatic locking function

This typeNo automatic locking function, always maintain the channel mode, and the door can be opened both inside and outside at any time. The simplest bead lock.

Toilet lock Privacy / Bath Lock

Inside - wave disappears outside - one-word key position
Normally, both indoor and outdoor Bobo can be unlocked. Pressing the indoor button can lock the outdoor Bobo. When the door is closed or the indoor wave is turned, the outdoor wave lock will be released. When locked, the emergency one-word batch/one-word silver door can be used to open the door outdoors.

Auto-locking feature

Does not have automatic locking function, the button must be pressed manually to lock outdoor. Suitable for single bathroom or bedroom.

Room door lock Entrance / Store Room

Inside - button Outside - key position
Normally, both indoor and outdoor Bobo can be unlocked. Pressing the indoor button can lock the outdoor Bobo. The outer wave lock will not be released when the door is closed, but the outdoor wave lock will be released when the indoor wave is turned. When locked, the key can be used to open the door outside.

Auto-locking trap

Automatic locking (long lock) function, if the indoor button is pressed and then the card is rotated to the "lock position", the outdoor lock will be long. Even if the indoor lock is turned or the key is used, the outdoor lock will not be released. When the door is closed, the door will be locked immediately, resulting in "automatic locking" (long lock).

Common traps of bead locks: Is it better to calculate the long-term "automatic lock"?

Many customers inquire about lock replacement. In fact, it is just because the "auto-locking function" of the bead lock in their home has been accidentally activated or the button is stuck. Especially for the door lock, the indoor button failed to pop out properly, causing the door to be closed every time and the outdoor button could not be turned, thinking that the lock was broken. In fact, it’s just a small setting issue!

How to judge whether to activate the "automatic locking function"?
Outdoor waves cannot be twistedThe outdoor wave is locked and cannot turn the latch
The key can be used to twist and drive the latch bolt normally.The key can open the door normally, which means the lock cylinder is not broken.
Twisting the indoor wave can drive the latch tongue normallyThe indoor wave function is normal
Twist the indoor wave but the button fails to pop up normallyKey symptoms: The indoor button is stuck in the automatic locking position.

Ultimate tutorial to release "automatic lock": press + rotate method

Reason: The button tenon is stuck in the position of the automatic locking function, causing the outdoor Bobo to remain in locking mode.

As long as you do the following 4 steps, the automatic lock state can be released immediately in 90% of cases:

  1. Step 1: In the door room, press the button (or knob) firmly and twist it clockwise (if there is no response when turning clockwise, you can change it counterclockwise).
  2. Step 2: While holding down the button and twisting it, twist the indoor Bobo (Bobo) with the other hand at the same time to test whether the button can pop out.
  3. Step 3: If it pops up successfully → the automatic locking function has been released and the outdoor wave returns to normal rotation.
    If it fails to pop up, repeat step 1 and step 2 about 2 to 3 times to change the rotation direction or strength.
  4. Step 4: Finally, open and close the door continuously to test to ensure that the outdoor wave can rotate freely and will not automatically lock again.
