Why do I need to bring a key when using an digital lock?

Behind smart technology, the last line of defense for physical keys

After many families have replaced digital locks, they find it convenient to use fingerprints, passwords, and mobile phones to unlock the locks, and gradually bury or even lose the physical keys. But you know what?Almost all compliant digital locks retain a mechanical keyhole, and this key is your only reliable backup plan in case of emergency. Let us explain to you today that when using an digital lock, you still need to carry a physical key with you.

1 Dead battery・Emergency rear door

digital locks rely on batteries to operate. Even if it is a flagship model, the battery will eventually run out. Most digital locks will issue a low battery warning in advance, but if you return from a business trip for a few weeks, or do not pay attention to the warning, the battery suddenly runs out——Without power, there is no way to use fingerprint, password, or Bluetooth to unlock the door.。

The only way to get started at this time is to usePhysical key. Some models are equipped with an external USB emergency power supply, but if you don’t have a urine bag, charging cable, or the interface is damaged, you still need to rely on the key. So, bring a key = make sure you are never locked out.

2 Electronic parts failure and emergency response

Any electronic product has a chance of breaking. The sensor, motherboard, and motor of an digital lock may suddenly malfunction due to moisture, static electricity, aging, or lightning strikes. Fingerprint recognition failed, the password panel did not respond, the Bluetooth connection was interrupted - these situations are not uncommon in the daily work of locksmiths.

When the electronic system completely crashes,The only physical way to open it is to use a mechanical key. Keeping the key gives you a way out, so you don't have to wait until midnight when no one is locking the door and you have no way to call for help.

Common myths and dismantling them one by one

    Myth 1: "The digital lock has a backup charging port, so a key is not needed."
    Fact: You need to bring your own USB cable and power bank for the charging port, and some models only support 9V square battery emergency, so it will be useless without one. The key system is the best solution.

    Myth 2: "I can call a locksmith to open the lock."
    Fact: If the digital lock breaks, calling a locksmith is a waste of time and money. Having the key will save you hundreds or even thousands in unlocking fees.

    Correct approach: Put the mechanical key that comes with the digital lock in a "reliable backup location" - such as in an office drawer, a family member's house, or inside the car. If you are worried about the safety of your home, you can also leave the key to a trusted neighbor for safekeeping. In short,Never bury all the keys in a place where you can’t even find them。

Pro Tips: How to properly manage digital lock keys?

    digital locks usually come with 2-5 mechanical keys, and at least one should be placed in a "safe place outside the house" (such as a private car, a company locker).
  • Use the key to insert and remove the lock cylinder every six months to ensure that the keyhole is smooth and prevent rust or dust from clogging it.
  • Replace the digital lock battery regularly (it is recommended to use alkaline batteries to avoid leakage), and replace the battery immediately when the low battery warning occurs to reduce the chance of using the key.

Summary: No matter how advanced the technology is, physical keys are still the "last door"

The convenience brought by digital locks is undeniable, but even the most stable electronic products may malfunction. A reliable mechanical key is small in size, does not require electricity, and is not subject to electronic interference. It is the most direct and safe solution when you face a power outage, machine failure, or natural disaster.

101 Locksmithing tips for you: Remember to treat the digital lock key as a "lifebuoy" and put it in an appropriate backup location. Make sure you have the backup key before going out (especially when traveling or leaving home for a long time).
