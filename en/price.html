How much does it cost to open a lock?

101 Locksmithing, transparent charging and honest service

1 Real-time quotation・Never cheat

Call/WhatsApp to ask for an instant quote
We insist on "quoting first, then construction". For any locksmithing needs, we will explain the charging details in advance, so that you can start the work first with a clear budget.

2 Integrity does not change the price

Unless the situation on site seriously does not match the description(if the lock cylinder is filled with glue or seriously damaged without honest notification), otherwiseNever change the quote. There will be no price hikes and no hidden charges.

3 Unsuccessful・No charge

Charge first if the lock is unlocked successfully. If the locksmith cannot technically open the door lock, no door-to-door fees, transportation fees or surcharges will be charged. This is our most practical guarantee for our guests.

Overview of charging structure

The unlocking fee is usually [Technical Difficulty] + [Time Period] + [Regional Location] + [bad weather] influence, the following table is the basic reference price for general unlocking.

Service Category Reference price (HKD) Description
Home mechanical door lock Starting from $500 Including ordinary metal gates, wooden doors and house doors, most traditional lock cylinders are technically opened.
Home digital lock Starting from $700 Samsung, Yale, Philips and other common brands of smart locks.
Commercial access control system (electric lock) Starting from $800 Unlocking of access control systems such as electromagnetic locks and electric bolt locks.
Car unlocking Starting from $800 Private cars, taxis, trucks, Japanese cars/European cars/rare cars are treated similarly.
Clip/Safe Starting from $800 Chubb, Yale, Safewell, Anbea and other brands.
Replace the lock cylinder Starting from $580 American locks, three-opening locks, European locks, tiger locks, all packaged.

Factors affecting price (honest and transparent)

  • time period: For emergency unlocking during the late night and early morning hours (23:00 - 07:00) or public holidays, the unlocking fee may be an additional $200-$600 more than during normal hours. Please explain the charging details to the customer when quoting.
  • Regional location: Remote areas such as restricted areas, outlying islands, etc. may incur additional charges, which will be clearly stated in the quotation.
  • Door lock level: The technical difficulty of high-security lock cylinders, multi-stage lock cylinders, anti-pry blade locks, etc. will increase, and the fees will be increased based on actual conditions, but must be communicated in advance.
  • Bad weather: During the No. 8 typhoon signal or black rainstorm warning, additional emergency service fees may be charged due to the safety of the master or traffic restrictions, but 24-hour support will still be maintained.
101 additional protection for unlocking: All surcharges will be clearly stated during the phone/WhatsApp quotation, and no unreasonable increase will be allowed after arrival.

※ The above charges are reference prices under general circumstances. The final price will be based on the quotation. We guarantee to confirm first before construction. 101 Locksmiths all 18 districts in Hong Kong with quick response and integrity comes first.
