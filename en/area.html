Regional Service FAQ

My location is not visible. Can I still check it?

Yes. The area page lists common service locations. If you are in a nearby housing estate, village house, industrial building or shopping mall, you can send a location and door lock photo query via WhatsApp.

What information do I need to provide when making inquiries?

It is recommended to provide the area, door lock photos, whether there is a key, whether the door is closed, and whether digital locks, metal gates or car locks are involved at the scene.

Is it possible to quote first and then arrange a visit?

Yes. The master will first make a preliminary assessment based on photos and conditions, and confirm the basic charging range before arriving at the site; special locks or on-site restrictions will be explained later.

Need to unlock or change the lock immediately?

You can directly call 66 101 101, or send the door lock photos, region and condition via WhatsApp. The master will first evaluate the lock type and quote.
