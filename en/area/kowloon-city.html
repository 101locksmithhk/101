Frequently Asked Questions about Locksmithing in Kowloon City District

Is door-to-door service available 24 hours a day in Kowloon City?

Yes. 101 Locksmithing provides 24-hour inquiry and door-to-door support. The actual arrival time will depend on the location of the master, transportation, and access arrangements for housing estates or commercial buildings. Providing an accurate address and a photo of the door lock when making an inquiry can speed up the evaluation.

What information do I need to provide before unlocking?

It is recommended to provide the front of the door lock, the door side lock tongue, the entire door, whether there is a key, whether anyone is in the house, and the identity of the resident or person in charge. If it is a company or shop, it is best to state the management office requirements at the same time.

Will it be necessary to destroy the door lock?

Not necessarily. The master will first determine the type of lock and the cause of the failure. If he can technically open it, he will give priority to retaining the original lock. It will only be recommended to replace the lock cylinder or door lock if the lock cylinder is seriously damaged, internal parts are scattered, or if the customer wishes to improve security.

How to know the fee?

Charging will be affected by the lock type, time, location, whether it is urgent, and whether parts need to be replaced. 101 Locksmithing recommends sending a photo via WhatsApp first. The master will provide a preliminary quotation based on the information and confirm it before arriving.

Need to unlock or change locks immediately in Kowloon City?

Call 66 101 101 directly, or WhatsApp to send photos, location and condition of the door lock. 101 Locksmith will first evaluate the lock type, processing methods and charges, and then arrange for a master to come to your door.
