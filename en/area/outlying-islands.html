Common locksmith services

Residential and village house door locks

Deal with situations such as forgetting to bring the key, leaving the key in the house, jamming the wooden door lock, malfunctioning lock cylinder, and failure of the metal gate lock to rotate. The master will first determine whether it can be technically opened and try to retain the original lock.

Lock replacement and digital lock

If you are moving, have lost your key, or have no power in the digital lock, or your fingerprint fails, or your password does not respond, you can first send photos of the door lock and model number. Lock cylinder dimensions, parts availability and quotation will be confirmed prior to arrival.

Shops and special locks

You can check first if the shop door locks, office door locks, car keys are left in the car or the folder cannot be opened. Commercial cases must be confirmed by the person in charge and arranged according to on-site management requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions about Locksmithing in Island Areas

Can door-to-door visits be arranged for all outlying island areas?

You can query first. Possible arrangements for Tung Chung and various outlying islands are subject to accurate address, transportation, sailing schedule, weather and instructor location confirmation; please do not assume that any island can be reached within a fixed time.

What information do I need to provide before making an inquiry?

Please provide the exact location, the front of the door lock, the door side deadbolt, the entire door, whether there is a key, and whether there is anyone on site or whether digital locks, metal gates or car locks are involved. This information helps to determine the type of lock, transportation arrangements and charges in advance.

How to quote for outlying island services?

Charges will be affected by lock type, processing time, parts requirements and transportation arrangements. It is recommended to send the photo and location via WhatsApp first, and the chef will confirm the feasible arrangements and quotation before departure.

Need to unlock or change locks immediately in the outlying island area?
