Common door lock problems and solutions in the area

Each area page should include content that is of practical use to visitors, rather than repeating the estate name multiple times.

Forgot to bring the key

First confirm which of the wooden door and metal gate is locked, and whether there is a backup key. Do not pry the door by yourself or use film to avoid damaging the door frame and lock tongue.

The gate lock is stuck

If the lock still cannot rotate even with the key, it may be due to wear of the lock cylinder, pressure on the lock tongue, or displacement of internal parts. Continuing to twist the key vigorously may cause the key to break in the keyhole.

The digital lock does not respond

First check the backup power supply method, battery and instructions in the manual. Do not re-enter your password to avoid triggering temporary locks or alarms.

Lock cylinder idling

The key can be turned but the lock bolt does not retract. It may be that the tail piece, connecting piece or lock body is faulty, and it needs to be checked according to the door lock structure.

Change the locks after moving

You can choose to replace only the lock cylinder or the entire set according to the condition of the existing door lock. New parts specifications, maintenance and total charges should be confirmed before formal construction.

Verify identity before unlocking

To ensure the safety of residents, the master will verify that guests have legal entry rights. The tenant, owner or company principal should prepare the appropriate certificates.
