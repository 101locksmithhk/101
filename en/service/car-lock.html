The mechanical key cannot open the door

The keyhole has not been used for a long time, the car key is worn, or the door lock mechanism is stuck. You need to determine the fault location first.

The remote control does not respond

It may involve the remote control battery, vehicle battery, synchronization setting or anti-theft system, and may not necessarily be a door lock problem.

Emergency safety:If there are young children, elders, pets, or immediate life-threatening danger in the car, you should contact 999 or relevant emergency services immediately, and should not just wait for general unlocking arrangements.

Query and processing flow

  1. Provide vehicle information:Make, model, year, location and which door is locked.
  2. Explain the car key situation:Whether the car keys are visible, whether they are all lost, whether the remote control responds, and whether there is a backup key.
  3. Confirm legal use:Prepare vehicle registration documents, driver's license or other verifiable information.
  4. Evaluation method:Confirm whether it can be processed and quote based on the car model, anti-theft design and on-site security conditions.

Charging and feasibility factors

  • Vehicle make, model, year and door lock/anti-theft mechanism.
  • The actual problem location on the door, trunk or hood.
  • Whether the vehicle parking environment is safe, legal and allows work to be performed.
  • Whether it only requires opening the door, or does it involve a faulty chip key, remote control or lock.
  • Location, transportation, time period and the range of car models that the master can handle.

Frequently Asked Questions about Car Unlocking

Can all car models be processed?

Not guaranteed. New and old car models, anti-theft designs and body structures are different, so accurate information must be provided first for confirmation.

Can I drive directly after opening the door?

If the chip car key is still in the car and functioning normally, it can usually be used in the original way; if the car key is completely lost or the anti-theft system fails, it belongs to another type of service.

Can underground parking be arranged?

It depends on the parking lot access, floor, management office rules, work space and signal conditions. You should explain it first when making inquiries.
