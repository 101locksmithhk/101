Compatibility check before digital lock installation

  1. Door body:Confirm the wooden door, metal gate, fire door or glass door, as well as the door thickness and opening direction.
  2. Original lock body:Measure the back distance, lock body length and width, lock tongue position and sky and ground hook.
  3. Usage:Confirm whether fingerprint, password, proximity card, mobile phone or remote functions are actually required.
  4. After-sales arrangements:Confirm the responsibility for digital lock brand maintenance, on-site inspection, parts supply and data reset.
Privacy Tips:The administrator password, fingerprint and application account of the smart door lock should be controlled by the customer; after installation, the administrator list should be checked and the temporary password should be deleted.

What does an digital lock service quote usually include?

  • Supply fees for digital lock products or designated smart door lock models.
  • Basic digital lock installation, original hole position modification and lock tongue alignment.
  • Filling holes, modifying the door body, changing the door frame or removing the special lock body should be separately specified.
  • Set the administrator, test the door opening method and provide basic usage instructions.

Frequently asked questions about digital lock installation and maintenance

Can all wooden doors be directly installed with digital locks?

No. Door thickness, door side space, lock body and original openings may limit the digital lock model and need to be measured first.

Does the digital lock have to be damaged to open the door if it has no power?

Most smart door locks are equipped with external emergency power supply or mechanical backup key, but the specific method must be specified according to the model.

Who provides digital lock maintenance?

Some digital locks can be maintained by the brand's original manufacturer. The actual life depends on the brand and model; you should confirm the registration, receipt and repair method before purchasing.
