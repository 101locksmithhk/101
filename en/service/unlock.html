What situations are suitable for querying unlocking?

Forgot to bring the key

The door lock of your residence, office or store is normal, but the key is left inside or lost.

Lock cylinder or bolt failure

The key can be inserted but cannot be turned, the door handle spins idle, or the door frame is displaced and the lock tongue is stuck.

The key is broken or has foreign matter

The key is broken in the keyhole, or the keyhole is blocked by foreign matter. Do not push deeper on your own.

Identity verification:Before unlocking, you need to confirm that the guest has the right to enter the relevant unit; the information required for residences, rentals, commercial places and vehicles may be different.

Locking service process

  1. provides information:Describe the region, door type, lock status and tried methods.
  2. Send photos:Take pictures of the entire outside of the door, the front of the lock and the deadbolt on the side of the door to help with preliminary judgment.
  3. Confirm arrangements:The master will explain the estimated charges according to the lock type, transportation, time period and tool needs.
  4. On-site verification:Confirm the identity and actual situation before deciding on technical start-up or other solutions.

What factors affect charging?

  • The structure of mechanical lock, digital lock, high security lock or multiple deadbolt.
  • The problem comes from the lock cylinder, deadbolt, door handle, door frame or electronic components.
  • Whether the keyhole or parts have been further damaged by using tools.
  • Service location, transportation, late night hours and whether parts need to be replaced.

The price on the home page is for reference only. The treatment plan and total cost confirmed before departure and after on-site inspection shall prevail.

Frequently asked questions about unlocking

Is it guaranteed to be completely lossless unlocking?

cannot be guaranteed. The master will give priority to evaluating technical openings, but lock malfunctions, damaged or high-security structures may require other methods; this should be explained before starting work.

What proof of identity should I prepare?

Depending on the location, this may include documents with address, lease, management confirmation, company authorization, or other information proving legal rights of use.

Is the arrival time fixed?

no. The arrival time is affected by the chef's location, traffic, time period and the order at that time. The arrangement confirmed when receiving the order shall prevail.
