Frequently asked questions

Forgot your password?

First confirm whether there is an administrative password, backup key, purchase receipt or original factory reset procedure, and then evaluate the password reset method.

The electronic panel does not respond

It may be that the battery is exhausted, the contacts are oxidized, or there is a problem with the panel or internal wiring. You should first check the external power supply method according to the electronic clip model.

Handle or latch stuck

If it still cannot rotate despite the correct password, it may be related to door pressure, force on the lock bolt, or internal transmission failure of the safe.

Common clamp brands and locks

The following are Yale, Chubb, Safewell, Safeon and Anbea that we have contacted. Different brands of electronic combination locks, key safes and mechanical locks have different structures. When inquiring about lock opening or repair in Hong Kong, please provide front and overall photos first.

Information required before the evaluation of Jiawan lock opening

  1. Brand and model:Take a complete picture of the nameplate, control panel, keyhole and clips.
  2. Fault process:Describes the last normal use, error prompts, sounds and attempted operations.
  3. Proof of ownership:Prepare purchase records, company authorizations, estate documents or other legal ownership information.
  4. Site conditions:Describe the size, weight, whether it is fixed into the wall/floor, and the surrounding work space.
Data security:There is no need to disclose the details of the items in the folder in advance, but it must be verified that they are legally owned or authorized to handle. Additional documentation may be required when disputes, estates or company assets are involved.

Jiawan maintenance plan and charging factors

  • Mechanical turntable, electronic keyboard, key, fingerprint or composite lock.
  • Clamp brand, model, security level, size and number of lock bolts.
  • Whether the clamp is required to be retained for continued use, and whether repair parts are still available.
  • Whether the electronic panel has been drilled, struck, twisted or removed in the past.
  • Moving, fixing and dismantling, stairs or narrow locations are another scope of work.

Any clip lock solution that requires drilling or destructive treatment should have a description of the location, impact, possibility of repair, and cost before commencing work.

Frequently asked questions about lock opening and repair

Can I reset my forgotten password?

Not necessarily. Some models can only be reset when the door is open; other models may require factory programming or administrative privileges.

Can the electronic clip be powered from the outside if there is no power?

Some electronic clips are equipped with external battery boxes, contacts or backup keys, but the methods vary depending on the brand and model. You should operate according to the manual or original manufacturer's instructions.

Can I continue to use the folder after it is opened?

Depends on the cause of the fault and the solution. If parts are damaged or must be opened destructively, the lock may need to be repaired or replaced.

First provide the folder brand, model and fault conditions

Do not repeatedly try incorrect passwords or forcefully twist the handle to avoid triggering the lock or increasing damage.
